Privacy

Last updated: 14 November 2026

This notice explains what happens to information when you visit sa-editions.com, the website of Sa Editions.

What we collect

The website has no accounts, no sign-up and no forms that send your details to us. We only receive what you choose to send: an e-mail or a WhatsApp message you write to us, which we use to answer your enquiry.

Stored in your browser

To remember your choices, the catalogue keeps a few small settings in your own browser's storage: the language, the unit (cm or inches), the pieces you mark as favourites, and the details you type into the trade form so you do not have to type them again. They never leave your device, and you can delete them at any time by clearing this site's data in your browser. The website sets no advertising or tracking cookies.

Services we use

  • Cloudflare hosts and delivers the website. Like any web host, it processes your IP address and basic request data to deliver pages and protect the site.
  • Google Fonts supplies some typefaces; your browser requests them from Google's servers.
  • YouTube (privacy-enhanced mode) shows the films on some piece pages, through youtube-nocookie.com.
  • WhatsApp, e-mail and Instagram open only when you tap their links; what you send there is covered by those services' own terms.

Certificates

When you fill in a certificate of authenticity, the certificate is drawn in your browser; the name you type is not sent to us.

Contact

For any question about your information, or to ask us to delete an enquiry you sent, write to sa.editions.info@gmail.com.
