The legs extend from the form like living roots, while the chair’s structure naturally adapts to the body, providing balance and comfort in seating.

Each version reveals a different quality of light, volume, and movement, creating a new relationship between form and space. Lighter colors soften the curves, making them appear more fluid and weightless, while giving the chair a calmer and more delicate presence. In contrast, darker tones emphasize shadows and depth, revealing a more sculptural and powerful expression of the same form. Thus, color is not merely a surface treatment, but an element that transforms our perception of weight, scale, movement, and the character of an object, presenting a new narrative each time from a single structure.
